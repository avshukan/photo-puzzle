# AI Image Generation Prompt for Photo Puzzle Presets

Use this prompt with an AI image generator (DALL·E 3, Midjourney, Stable Diffusion,
or similar) to create replacement preset images for the puzzle game.

## Requirements

- **Size**: 800 × 600 px (4:3 landscape)
- **Style**: vibrant, high-contrast, photorealistic photo
- **Audience**: kids and families — friendly, colorful, no scary or violent content
- **Format**: JPEG, saved as `preset-1.jpg` … `preset-5.jpg` in `src/assets/presets/`
- Avoid text, watermarks, and logos inside the image

## Tips

- Ask the generator for **"vibrant, saturated colors"** and **"photorealistic"**
  to get images that look great as puzzle pieces.
- Regenerate until you get a version with good detail spread across the whole
  image (avoid large uniform-color areas that make the puzzle too easy).
- Resize/crop to exactly 800 × 600 px before placing the file in the repo.
